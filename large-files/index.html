Large files.

xlsx-for-ai reads workbooks up to 10 MB for free, like everything else it does. Files past that run on the paid plan — currently US $25 per year, billed annually.

Why there's a limit on the free tier

Reading a workbook into memory, evaluating its formulas, and writing a reviewed copy all hold the file — and intermediate working copies of it — in RAM at once. For most workbooks that's comfortable. Past about 10 MB it stops being comfortable on the memory we provision for a free-tier job, so we ask you to move to the paid plan rather than risk a slow or flaky result.

Larger files run on the paid plan

Everything up to 10 MB stays free, same as everywhere else on xlsx-for-ai. Files above that run on the paid plan — currently US $25 per year, billed annually — which covers larger workbooks.

Free Shopify → Google feed builder

Turn your Shopify products export into a Google Shopping feed — free.

Export your products from Shopify, upload the CSV, and add two things Google needs that aren't in the file — your store's web address and your currency. You get back a Google Merchant Center product feed, with any product missing a title, price, or image listed for you instead of quietly dropped.

Free · no signup · your file is never stored
shopify-google-feed · xlsx-for-ai

What you upload

A Shopify products export. In your Shopify admin go to Products → Export and choose the plain CSV. It has one row per product and variant, with columns like Title, Body, Variant Price, Image Src, and Variant Barcode — the fields a Google offer is built from.

The two things you add

Your store's web address becomes the link for every product, so shoppers who click through in Google land on your store. Your currency (a three-letter code like USD or GBP) is attached to every price, because Google won't accept a price without one. Neither lives in the export, so you supply them once here.

What you get back

A tab-delimited product feed with Google's expected columns — id, title, description, link, image_link, availability, price, and the rest. In Google Merchant Center, add it as a feed and upload this file.

Nothing is silently dropped

A product that can't become a valid offer — no title, no price, or no image — is excluded and listed in the results, along with the reason. Blank optional attributes are flagged too, so you know exactly what Google will and won't see.

How it works

The page runs the same conversion your AI agent would call through our tools. It reads your export in memory and never stores it — your original CSV is untouched; you download a new feed file, so nothing is ever lost.

Want this inside your AI agent, on your own machine, with no upload at all? Install xlsx-for-ai and ask Claude or Cursor to "turn this Shopify products export into a Google Shopping feed."

Free Shopify → Amazon feed builder

Turn your Shopify products export into an Amazon feed — free.

Export your products from Shopify, upload the CSV, and get back an Amazon flat-file inventory feed you can upload in Seller Central. We map your titles, prices, and identifiers into Amazon's template columns — and any product missing a required identifier is listed for you, never guessed.

Free · no signup · your file is never stored
shopify-amazon-feed · xlsx-for-ai

What you upload

A Shopify products export. In your Shopify admin go to Products → Export and choose the plain CSV — that's the file this tool reads. It has one row per product (and per variant), with columns like Title, Variant Price, Variant SKU, and Variant Barcode.

What you get back

A tab-delimited Amazon inventory file — the same shape as Amazon's flat-file Template sheet. Each eligible variant becomes one listing row with its title, price, quantity, and identifier mapped into the columns Amazon expects. Upload it in Seller Central under Inventory → Add Products via Upload.

Products without an identifier are listed, not guessed

Amazon requires a real product identifier (UPC, EAN, or GTIN) to match or create a listing. If a product doesn't carry one, we leave it out of the feed and list it in the results so you can add the identifier yourself — we never invent one, because a fabricated barcode is a listing that gets suppressed.

How it works

The page runs the same conversion your AI agent would call through our tools. It reads your export in memory and never stores it — your original CSV is untouched; you download a new feed file, so nothing is ever lost.

Want this inside your AI agent, on your own machine, with no upload at all? Install xlsx-for-ai and ask Claude or Cursor to "turn this Shopify products export into an Amazon feed."
